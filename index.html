Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions.

How can I contact support?

Email our support team at belalscg@gmail.com, or tap Contact Support anywhere on this page. We read every message and reply as soon as possible.

I cannot log in. What should I do?
  • Check that you have a stable internet connection.
  • Make sure you're signing in with the same method and account you used before.
  • Update the app to the latest version from the App Store.
  • Close the app completely and open it again.

If you still can't sign in, contact support with the email address or sign-in method linked to your account. Never send us your password.

The app is not working correctly. What should I do?

First, close the app completely and reopen it. Then make sure you're running the latest version of the app and iOS, and restart your device if needed. If the problem continues, please contact support so we can look into it.

How can I report a problem?

Email support and include as much detail as you can:

  • Your device model and iOS version
  • The app version (shown in the App Store or the app's settings)
  • What happened and the steps that lead to the problem
  • Screenshots or a screen recording, if possible
How can I send feedback?

We'd love to hear from you. Use the Send feedback link above or email us with your suggestions and ideas. Every message helps us improve the app.
